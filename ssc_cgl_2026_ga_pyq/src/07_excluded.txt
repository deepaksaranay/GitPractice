## 7.3 Pattern Lessons From 2026 (Useful for JE, CPO, CHSL 2026)
- Constitutional Articles came in clusters (one shift had about five questions on Articles 243 and 131A). Learn Articles group-wise, including omitted ones (131A, 144A, 226A).
- The economy section repeated 1991 reforms and Five-Year Plans across different dates (1 Oct and 28 Oct).
- Culture questions favoured the North-East (Mizoram, Assam) and the East (Odisha, Bengal).
- Books and authors, musicians and instruments, and sports firsts/terms formed most of 'static GK'.
- General Science was the largest single block (5–7 questions), so do not skip it for current affairs.
