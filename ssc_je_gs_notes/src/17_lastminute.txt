## 17.4 Exam-Day Strategy for GA
- Do GA first; spend at most 10–12 minutes. Answer what you know immediately; mark doubtful questions and come back only if time remains.
- Eliminate options. If two options remain and you know the concept area, a guess is reasonable; if you have no idea, skip (negative marking).
- Watch for word traps: 'first' vs 'last', 'largest' vs 'longest', 'founded' vs 'built', 'Governor-General of Bengal' vs 'of India', 'oldest' vs 'first'.
- Read current affairs of the last 6–8 months separately: awards, sports winners, appointments, summits, schemes, space missions, indices and rankings.
TIP: Revise this book with the "three-pass" method: pass 1 – full chapters; pass 2 – only yellow strips and blue boxes (Appendices A & B); pass 3 – the recall bank and this chapter on the last two days.
WARN: Do not learn shaky "facts" from unverified social-media PDFs in the last week. If two sources disagree, prefer NCERT, official government websites and the Constitution's text.
