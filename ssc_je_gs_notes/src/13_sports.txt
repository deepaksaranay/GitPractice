# Static GK I – Sports

## 13.1 Olympics
- Ancient Olympics: Olympia, Greece, 776 BCE. Modern Olympics: founded by Pierre de Coubertin (IOC, 1894; HQ Lausanne, Switzerland); first Games Athens 1896. Motto: 'Citius, Altius, Fortius' (Faster, Higher, Stronger) – 'Communiter' (Together) added in 2021. Five rings (blue, yellow, black, green, red) represent five continents. Olympic Day 23 June.
| Year | Host | India's highlight |

| 1928 | Amsterdam | First hockey gold (Dhyan Chand) |
| 1952 | Helsinki | K.D. Jadhav – bronze (wrestling), first individual medal of independent India |
| 1980 | Moscow | Hockey gold (8th and last hockey gold) |
| 1996 | Atlanta | Leander Paes – bronze (tennis) |
| 2000 | Sydney | Karnam Malleswari – bronze (weightlifting), first Indian woman medallist |
| 2004 | Athens | Rajyavardhan Rathore – silver (shooting, double trap) |
| 2008 | Beijing | Abhinav Bindra – gold (10 m air rifle), first individual gold |
| 2012 | London | 6 medals (Sushil Kumar silver, Vijay Kumar silver, Saina, Mary Kom, Gagan Narang, Yogeshwar Dutt) |
| 2016 | Rio | P.V. Sindhu – silver (badminton), Sakshi Malik – bronze (wrestling) |
| 2020 (held 2021) | Tokyo | 7 medals – best haul then; Neeraj Chopra – gold (javelin, first athletics gold); Mirabai Chanu silver; hockey bronze |
| 2024 | Paris | 6 medals (Neeraj silver; Manu Bhaker 2 bronze – first Indian with two medals at one Games; Aman Sehrawat bronze; Swapnil Kusale bronze; hockey bronze) |

| 2032 | Brisbane | Upcoming |
- India's 8 Olympic hockey golds: 1928, 1932, 1936, 1948, 1952, 1956, 1964, 1980. Dhyan Chand – 'Wizard of Hockey'; his birthday 29 August = National Sports Day; Khel Ratna renamed Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna (2021).
- Asian Games: first held in New Delhi, 1951 (also 1982). Commonwealth Games: Delhi 2010. Paralympics: Tokyo 2020 – Avani Lekhara first Indian woman Paralympic gold; Paris 2024 – India's best (29 medals).
F: Modern Olympics – Athens 1896 (Coubertin) | IOC HQ – Lausanne | First individual gold – Abhinav Bindra 2008 | First athletics gold – Neeraj Chopra 2021 | Hockey golds – 8 | National Sports Day – 29 Aug
