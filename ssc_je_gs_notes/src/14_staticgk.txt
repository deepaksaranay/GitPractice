## 14.4 Awards and Honours
- Bharat Ratna (instituted 2 January 1954): highest civilian award; maximum 3 per year (exceeded in 2024 with 5). First recipients (1954): C. Rajagopalachari, S. Radhakrishnan, C.V. Raman. First posthumous – Lal Bahadur Shastri (1966); first woman – Indira Gandhi (1971); first foreigner – Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (1987, 'Frontier Gandhi'); Nelson Mandela (1990); first sportsperson – Sachin Tendulkar (2014); 2024 awardees – Karpoori Thakur, L.K. Advani, P.V. Narasimha Rao, Chaudhary Charan Singh, M.S. Swaminathan. It is a medallion shaped like a peepal leaf.
- Padma awards (1954): Padma Vibhushan > Padma Bhushan > Padma Shri; announced on Republic Day eve.
- Gallantry (wartime): Param Vir Chakra (highest; first – Major Somnath Sharma, 1947, posthumous; designed by Savitri Khanolkar), Maha Vir Chakra, Vir Chakra. Peacetime: Ashoka Chakra, Kirti Chakra, Shaurya Chakra.
- Literature: Jnanpith (1961; first awarded 1965 – G. Sankara Kurup, Malayalam, 'Odakkuzhal'; first woman – Ashapurna Devi, 1976, Bengali); Sahitya Akademi Award (1954); Saraswati Samman (K.K. Birla Foundation, 1991); Vyas Samman (Hindi); Booker Prize (UK); Pulitzer Prize (USA, journalism & literature).
- Cinema: Dadasaheb Phalke Award (1969; highest in Indian cinema; first – Devika Rani, 'First Lady of Indian Cinema'); National Film Awards; Oscars (Academy Awards, USA – first Indian winner Bhanu Athaiya, costume design, Gandhi 1983; Satyajit Ray honorary Oscar 1992; A.R. Rahman – 2 Oscars (Slumdog Millionaire, 2009); 'Naatu Naatu' (RRR) – best original song 2023; 'The Elephant Whisperers' – documentary short 2023).
- Nobel Prize (1901; Alfred Nobel – inventor of dynamite; awarded in Stockholm, Peace Prize in Oslo; economics added 1968 (Sveriges Riksbank prize)). Indians/Indian-origin laureates: Rabindranath Tagore (Literature 1913 – first Indian & first Asian), C.V. Raman (Physics 1930), Har Gobind Khorana (Medicine 1968), Mother Teresa (Peace 1979), S. Chandrasekhar (Physics 1983), Amartya Sen (Economics 1998), V.S. Naipaul (Literature 2001), Venkatraman Ramakrishnan (Chemistry 2009), Kailash Satyarthi (Peace 2014, with Malala Yousafzai), Abhijit Banerjee (Economics 2019).
- Ramon Magsaysay Award (Philippines; 'Asia's Nobel'): Vinoba Bhave (first Indian, 1958), Mother Teresa, Kiran Bedi, Arvind Kejriwal, Ravish Kumar, etc.
- Other: Templeton Prize (religion), Pritzker (architecture; B.V. Doshi first Indian, 2018), Abel Prize & Fields Medal (mathematics; Fields every 4 years, under 40 – Manjul Bhargava, Indian-origin, 2014), Kalinga Prize (UNESCO, popularisation of science; instituted by Biju Patnaik), Gandhi Peace Prize (1995; first – Julius Nyerere), Indira Gandhi Peace Prize, Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize (science; now replaced by Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar from 2023, highest – Vigyan Ratna), Borlaug Award (agriculture), Tansen Samman (music), Kalidas Samman (MP), Lal Bahadur Shastri award, Hoover Medal (engineering for humanity).
F: Bharat Ratna 1954 (first: Rajaji, Radhakrishnan, Raman) | Jnanpith first – G. Sankara Kurup 1965 | Phalke first – Devika Rani 1969 | First Indian Nobel – Tagore 1913 | First Oscar – Bhanu Athaiya | Param Vir Chakra first – Somnath Sharma
PYQ-TREND: First Bharat Ratna recipients, first woman/foreigner/sportsperson. Jnanpith first recipient and language. Dadasaheb Phalke first recipient. Indian Nobel laureates with fields and years. Magsaysay as Asia's Nobel. Fields medal field (mathematics). Pritzker (architecture). Gallantry awards order. Recent Bharat Ratna (2024).
WARN: Every year new Padma, Phalke, Jnanpith, Nobel and Booker winners are announced. Check the last year's list from current affairs.
