# Solved Numericals – Set 3 (Casting, Welding, Forming, Machining)
SSC-style production problems solved step by step (no options).

## 25C.1 Casting
### Problem 1 – Shrinkage allowance
A grey cast iron casting is to be 500 mm long. Find the pattern length (shrinkage 10 mm/m).
F: Allowance = 10 × 0.5 = 5 mm → pattern = 505 mm
### Problem 2 – Chvorinov's rule
A 100 mm cube solidifies in 5 min. Find the solidification time of a sphere of the same volume and same material/mould.
F: Cube: V/A = a/6 = 16.67 mm;  sphere: r = (3V/4π)^(1/3) = 62.04 mm → V/A = r/3 = 20.68 mm
F: t = 5 × (20.68/16.67)² = 5 × 1.539 = 7.7 min (sphere freezes slower)
### Problem 3 – Velocity at sprue base and filling time
The effective sprue height is 200 mm, gate area 200 mm², mould volume 10⁶ mm³ (top gating). Find gate velocity and filling time.
F: V = √(2gh) = √(2 × 9810 × 200) = 1981 mm/s ≈ 1.98 m/s
F: t = V_mould/(A_g V) = 10⁶/(200 × 1981) = 2.52 s
### Problem 4 – Sprue taper
The sprue bottom (head 250 mm) has area 100 mm²; the sprue top is at a head of 50 mm. Find the top area to avoid aspiration.
F: A_top/A_bottom = √(h_bottom/h_top) = √(250/50) = 2.236 → A_top = 223.6 mm²
### Problem 5 – Centrifugal casting speed
Find the rotational speed for a pipe of inner diameter 200 mm with G-factor 60.
F: ω²r = 60g → ω = √(60 × 9.81/0.1) = 76.7 rad/s → N = 732 rpm

## 25C.2 Welding
### Problem 6 – Heat input
Arc welding at 25 V, 200 A and travel speed 5 mm/s with 80% efficiency. Find the heat input per mm.
F: H = ηVI/v = 0.8 × 25 × 200/5 = 800 J/mm
### Problem 7 – Resistance spot welding
Current 10,000 A, resistance 100 μΩ, time 0.1 s. Find the heat generated.
F: H = I²Rt = 10⁸ × 10⁻⁴ × 0.1 = 1000 J
### Problem 8 – Fillet weld strength
A 10 mm fillet weld 100 mm long (two parallel welds) has allowable shear stress 80 MPa. Find the load capacity.
F: Throat = 0.707 × 10 = 7.07 mm;  P = 2 × 7.07 × 100 × 80 = 113 kN
### Problem 9 – Arc length and voltage
The arc characteristic is V = 20 + 4l (l in mm). Find the voltage for a 3 mm arc.
F: V = 20 + 4 × 3 = 32 V

## 25C.3 Metal Forming
### Problem 10 – Maximum draft in rolling
Roll radius 300 mm, μ = 0.1. Find the maximum possible draft and the bite angle.
F: Δh_max = μ²R = 0.01 × 300 = 3 mm;  cosα = 1 − 3/600 = 0.995 → α = 5.73° (tanα ≈ 0.1 = μ ✓)
### Problem 11 – Exit velocity in rolling
A strip 20 mm thick enters at 1 m/s and leaves 15 mm thick (width constant). Find the exit velocity.
F: h₀v₀ = h_f v_f → v_f = 20 × 1/15 = 1.33 m/s
### Problem 12 – Blanking force and tool sizes
A 50 mm diameter blank is cut from 2 mm sheet (τ = 300 MPa); clearance 5% of t per side. Find force and tool sizes.
F: F = πDtτ = π × 50 × 2 × 300 = 94.2 kN
F: Clearance = 0.1 mm → die = 50 mm, punch = 50 − 0.2 = 49.8 mm
### Problem 13 – Punching a hole
A 20 mm hole is punched in the same sheet. Find punch and die sizes.
F: Punch = 20 mm;  die = 20 + 0.2 = 20.2 mm
### Problem 14 – Deep drawing blank
Find the blank diameter for a cup of 50 mm diameter and 40 mm height. Can it be drawn in one stage?
F: D = √(d² + 4dh) = √(2500 + 8000) = 102.5 mm;  drawing ratio = 102.5/50 = 2.05 > ≈ 2 → needs two draws
### Problem 15 – Extrusion pressure
A billet of 100 mm diameter is extruded to 50 mm (flow stress 100 MPa). Find ideal pressure and force.
F: R = (100/50)² = 4;  p = σ ln R = 100 × 1.386 = 138.6 MPa;  F = 138.6 × 7854 = 1.09 MN
### Problem 16 – Wire drawing stress
Wire is drawn from 5 mm to 4 mm (flow stress 300 MPa). Find the ideal drawing stress. Is it feasible?
F: σ_d = 300 ln(25/16) = 300 × 0.446 = 133.9 MPa < 300 MPa → feasible (reduction = 36%)

## 25C.4 Metal Cutting
### Problem 17 – Turning time and MRR
A 50 mm bar is turned at 500 rpm, feed 0.2 mm/rev, depth 2 mm, length 200 mm. Find V, time and MRR.
F: V = π × 50 × 500/1000 = 78.5 m/min;  t = 200/(0.2 × 500) = 2 min
F: MRR = V f d = 78,540 × 0.2 × 2 = 31,416 mm³/min
### Problem 18 – Merchant's analysis
Orthogonal cutting: α = 10°, uncut thickness 0.2 mm, chip thickness 0.5 mm, F_c = 1200 N, F_t = 500 N. Find shear angle, friction coefficient, shear force and shear strain.
F: r = 0.4;  tanφ = 0.4 cos10°/(1 − 0.4 sin10°) = 0.3939/0.9305 = 0.4233 → φ = 22.9°
F: μ = (F_t + F_c tanα)/(F_c − F_t tanα) = (500 + 211.6)/(1200 − 88.2) = 0.640 → β = 32.6°
F: F_s = F_c cosφ − F_t sinφ = 1105.1 − 194.9 = 910 N
F: γ = cotφ + tan(φ − α) = 2.362 + 0.230 = 2.59
- Merchant's theory would predict φ = 45° + 5° − 16.3° = 33.7°; the difference shows the theory's limitations.
### Problem 19 – Taylor's tool life
Tool life equation VT^0.25 = 300. Find the speed for 60 min life, and tool life at 150 m/min.
F: V = 300/60^0.25 = 300/2.783 = 107.8 m/min;  T = (300/150)^4 = 16 min
### Problem 20 – Taylor constants from two tests
Tool life is 60 min at 100 m/min and 12 min at 150 m/min. Find n and C.
F: n = ln(150/100)/ln(60/12) = 0.4055/1.6094 = 0.252;  C = 100 × 60^0.252 = 280.6
### Problem 21 – Percentage change in tool life
For n = 0.25, if cutting speed is increased by 20%, find the change in tool life.
F: T₂/T₁ = (V₁/V₂)^(1/n) = (1/1.2)⁴ = 0.482 → tool life falls by about 52%
### Problem 22 – Drilling time
A 20 mm drill makes a 40 mm deep hole at 400 rpm, feed 0.2 mm/rev. Find time (approach 0.3D).
F: t = (40 + 6)/(0.2 × 400) = 0.575 min = 34.5 s
### Problem 23 – Milling time
A 100 mm slab cutter with 10 teeth runs at 200 rpm, feed 0.1 mm/tooth, depth 5 mm, work length 300 mm. Find time.
F: f_m = 0.1 × 10 × 200 = 200 mm/min;  approach = √(5 × 95) = 21.8 mm
F: t = (300 + 21.8)/200 = 1.61 min
### Problem 24 – Indexing
Find the indexing for 36 and 52 divisions (plates with 18- and 39-hole circles).
F: 36: 40/36 = 1 1/9 = 1 turn + 2 holes in 18-hole circle;  52: 40/52 = 10/13 = 30 holes in 39-hole circle
### Problem 25 – Shaper cutting speed and time
Stroke 200 mm, 30 strokes/min, return/cutting time ratio 2/3; job width 150 mm, feed 1 mm/stroke. Find average cutting speed and time.
F: V = LN(1 + m)/1000 = 200 × 30 × 1.667/1000 = 10 m/min;  strokes = 150 → time = 150/30 = 5 min
### Problem 26 – Surface roughness from feed and nose radius
Feed 0.2 mm/rev, nose radius 0.8 mm. Find peak-to-valley roughness and approximate Ra.
F: h = f²/(8R) = 0.04/6.4 = 6.25 μm;  Ra ≈ h/4 ≈ 1.56 μm
