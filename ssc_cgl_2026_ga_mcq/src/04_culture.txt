# Part B – Art & Culture Practice MCQs

## 4.1 Dances
M: Sattriya, a classical dance, belongs to: || Manipur || Assam || Odisha || Kerala || b || Introduced by Shankardev in the sattras; recognised as classical in 2000.
M: Kuchipudi originated in: || Tamil Nadu || Karnataka || Andhra Pradesh || Kerala || c || Named after a village in Krishna district; Tarangam is danced on a brass plate.
M: Which two classical dances belong to Kerala? || Kathakali and Mohiniyattam || Kathakali and Kuchipudi || Odissi and Mohiniyattam || Bharatanatyam and Kathakali || a || Kathakali – dance-drama; Mohiniyattam – solo female lasya.
M: The 'tribhanga' posture is characteristic of: || Kathak || Odissi || Manipuri || Bharatanatyam || b || Three bends of the body – head, torso, knees.
M: Birju Maharaj was an exponent of: || Kathak || Kathakali || Odissi || Bharatanatyam || a || Lucknow gharana.
M: Kelucharan Mohapatra is associated with: || Kuchipudi || Odissi || Manipuri || Sattriya || b || He revived Odissi in the 20th century.
M: Lavani is a folk dance of: || Gujarat || Maharashtra || Rajasthan || Goa || b || Performed to the beat of the dholki; Tamasha is the related folk theatre.
M: Yakshagana is associated with: || Kerala || Karnataka || Tamil Nadu || Andhra Pradesh || b || Dance-drama of coastal Karnataka.
M: Garba and Dandiya Raas are folk dances of: || Rajasthan || Gujarat || Madhya Pradesh || Haryana || b || Performed during Navratri; Garba was added to UNESCO's ICH list in 2023.
M: Hojagiri is a folk dance of: || Tripura || Manipur || Nagaland || Sikkim || a || Performed by the Reang (Bru) community, balancing on pitchers.
M: Ghoomar is a folk dance of: || Punjab || Rajasthan || Haryana || Himachal Pradesh || b || Originated with the Bhil tribe; Kalbelia (Rajasthan) is on UNESCO's ICH list.
M: Theyyam is a ritual dance of: || Karnataka || Kerala || Goa || Tamil Nadu || b || North Kerala (Kannur, Kasaragod).
M: Nati, which holds a Guinness record, is a dance of: || Uttarakhand || Himachal Pradesh || Jammu & Kashmir || Sikkim || b || Kullu Nati.
M: Raut Nacha and Panthi are dances of: || Odisha || Chhattisgarh || Jharkhand || Madhya Pradesh || b || Panthi – Satnami community.
M: Which form of Chhau is performed without masks? || Purulia || Seraikela || Mayurbhanj || All use masks || c || Mayurbhanj (Odisha). Purulia (West Bengal) and Seraikela (Jharkhand) use masks.
