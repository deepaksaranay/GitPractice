# Part B – Five GA Mock Sets (2026 Pattern)
Each set has 25 questions in the 2026 CGL proportion (science and static GK heavy, polity/history/economy/geography mixed). Time yourself: 15 minutes per set, +2 for a right answer, −0.50 for a wrong one.
