## 6.1 India – Physical

M: Shipki La pass is in: || Himachal Pradesh || Uttarakhand || Ladakh || Sikkim || a || The Sutlej enters India here.
M: Which plain is formed by new alluvium? || Bhabar || Terai || Bhangar || Khadar || d || Khadar – floodplains, fertile. Bhangar – old alluvium with kankar.
M: Chilika Lake, the largest brackish-water lagoon of India, is in: || Andhra Pradesh || Odisha || Tamil Nadu || West Bengal || b || One of India's first Ramsar sites (1981).

## 6.4 World and Physical Geography
M: The hottest planet in the solar system is: || Mercury || Venus || Mars || Jupiter || b || Its thick CO₂ atmosphere traps heat (greenhouse effect).
M: The largest moon in the solar system is: || Titan || Ganymede || Europa || Moon || b || Ganymede, a moon of Jupiter.
M: Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet in: || 2000 || 2006 || 2010 || 2015 || b || By the International Astronomical Union.
M: The ozone layer lies in the: || Troposphere || Stratosphere || Mesosphere || Thermosphere || b || About 15–35 km; absorbs UV radiation.
M: Meteors burn up in which layer of the atmosphere? || Troposphere || Stratosphere || Mesosphere || Exosphere || c || The mesosphere is also the coldest layer.
M: The Chinook is a warm local wind of: || Alps || Rocky Mountains || Andes || Sahara || b || Called the 'snow eater'. Foehn – Alps; Sirocco – Sahara to Italy.
M: Tropical cyclones are called 'typhoons' in the: || Atlantic Ocean || Western Pacific / China Sea || Indian Ocean || Caribbean || b || Hurricanes – Atlantic/Caribbean; cyclones – Indian Ocean; willy-willies – Australia.
M: The deepest point in the world's oceans is the: || Puerto Rico Trench || Java Trench || Challenger Deep, Mariana Trench || Tonga Trench || c || About 10,994 m, western Pacific.
M: The Gulf Stream is a: || Cold current of the Pacific || Warm current of the Atlantic || Cold current of the Atlantic || Warm current of the Indian Ocean || b || Keeps north-west Europe warm; meets the cold Labrador current near Newfoundland (Grand Banks).
M: The longest river in the world is the: || Amazon || Nile || Yangtze || Mississippi || b || About 6,650 km. The Amazon has the largest discharge.
M: The Strait of Gibraltar connects the Atlantic Ocean with the: || Red Sea || Mediterranean Sea || Black Sea || Arabian Sea || b || Separates Spain from Morocco.
M: The Strait of Hormuz connects the Persian Gulf with the: || Red Sea || Gulf of Oman || Arabian Sea directly || Caspian Sea || b || A key oil shipping route.
M: The 38th Parallel is the boundary between: || USA and Canada || North and South Korea || China and Mongolia || Germany and Poland || b || The 49th Parallel separates the USA and Canada.
M: 'Pampas' are temperate grasslands of: || Australia || Argentina || South Africa || Russia || b || Prairies – N. America; Steppes – Eurasia; Veld – South Africa; Downs – Australia.
M: The driest desert in the world is the: || Sahara || Atacama || Gobi || Kalahari || b || Chile.
M: The parliament of Japan is called the: || Knesset || Diet || Duma || Majlis || b || Knesset – Israel; Duma – Russia; Majlis – Iran.
M: The currency of Bhutan is the: || Rupee || Taka || Ngultrum || Kyat || c || Taka – Bangladesh; Kyat – Myanmar; Rufiyaa – Maldives.
M: 1° of longitude corresponds to a time difference of: || 1 minute || 4 minutes || 15 minutes || 1 hour || b || 360° = 24 h → 15° = 1 h.
M: The longest day in the Northern Hemisphere is: || 21 March || 21 June || 23 September || 22 December || b || Summer solstice; the Sun is overhead at the Tropic of Cancer.
M: The crust–mantle boundary is called the: || Gutenberg discontinuity || Moho discontinuity || Conrad discontinuity || Lehmann discontinuity || b || Gutenberg is the mantle–core boundary.
