## 30.3 Comparison Quick-Hits
| Topic | Remember |
| Same r, same heat input | η: Otto > Dual > Diesel |
| Same max p and T | η: Diesel > Dual > Otto |
| Minimum compression work | Isothermal (polytropic n smaller = less work) |
| Carnot vs Rankine (vapour) | Rankine practical: complete condensation, small pump work |
| Reheat vs regeneration | Reheat → dryness; regeneration → efficiency |
| Fire-tube vs water-tube | Low p, small vs high p, large; water-tube for power plants |
| Impulse vs reaction | Pressure drop only in nozzles vs in both fixed and moving blades |
| SI vs CI knock | Late autoignition of end gas vs long ignition delay at start |
| Remedies for SI knock | High octane, low r, retard spark – these cause knock in CI |
| VCR vs VAR | Work-driven, high COP vs heat-driven, low COP |
| Dropwise vs filmwise | Dropwise higher h; filmwise used for design |
| Parallel vs counter flow | Counter flow: higher LMTD, smaller area, higher effectiveness |
| Biot vs Nusselt | k of solid vs k of fluid |
| Free vs forced convection | Gr vs Re governs |

## 30.4 Traps Toppers Avoid
- Always convert temperatures to kelvin in efficiency, COP, entropy and radiation formulas.
- Work done by a system is positive in Q = ΔU + W; work done on it is negative.
- Steady-flow work uses c_p ΔT; non-flow (closed) adiabatic work uses c_v ΔT.
- Four-stroke IP uses N/2 power strokes per minute; two-stroke uses N.
- Equivalent evaporation is referred to 'from and at 100 °C' – divide by 2257, not 2500.
- COP of a heat pump is one more than COP of a refrigerator between the same temperatures – not double.
- Clearance reduces volumetric efficiency but does not change the ideal work per kg of air delivered.
- In LMTD, define ΔT₁ and ΔT₂ according to flow arrangement.
- Critical radius: insulation increases heat loss until r = r_c – check whether the pipe radius is above or below r_c.
- Lumped analysis: L_c = V/A (sphere R/3), not the radius itself.
- In refrigerant numbering, add 90 to get C-H-F atoms; R-7XX = 700 + molecular mass.
- Wet-bulb temperature lies between DBT and DPT; all three are equal only at 100% RH.

## 30.5 Final 10-Minute Checklist
- Laws: zeroth (temperature), first (energy), second (direction/quality), third (zero entropy at 0 K).
- Cycles: Carnot, Otto, Diesel, Dual, Brayton, Stirling, Ericsson, Rankine, Bell–Coleman, VCR – know diagrams and efficiencies.
- IC engines: valve timing, knock, octane/cetane, Morse test, Willan's line, performance formulae.
- Steam: boiler types and mountings/accessories, nozzles and critical pressure, turbine compounding and blade efficiency, condensers and cooling towers.
- Power plants: nuclear components, load curve factors, combined cycle.
- Compressors: multistage, intercooling, volumetric efficiency; rotary compressors, surging and choking.
- RAC: TR, VCR cycle and effects, refrigerant numbering, VAR (Electrolux), psychrometric processes, SHF and BPF.
- Heat transfer: conduction resistances, critical radius, fins, lumped analysis, dimensionless numbers, boiling, radiation laws, LMTD/NTU.
