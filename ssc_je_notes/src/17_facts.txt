## 17.4 Paired Comparisons (Frequent Twisters)
| A | B |
| Governor – mean speed (long term) | Flywheel – cyclic fluctuation (within cycle) |
| Toughness – area under whole curve | Resilience – area up to elastic limit |
| Hot working – above recrystallisation T | Cold working – below recrystallisation T |
| Blanking – clearance on punch | Punching – clearance on die |
| TIG – non-consumable W electrode | MIG – consumable wire |
| DCSP – deeper penetration | DCRP – cleaning action, thin sheet |
| Annealing – furnace cooling | Normalising – air cooling |
| Austempering – bainite | Martempering – martensite with less distortion |
| Impulse turbine – pressure constant over moving blades | Reaction turbine – pressure drops in moving blades too |
| Pelton – high head, low Ns | Kaplan – low head, high Ns |
| Centrifugal pump – priming needed | Reciprocating pump – self-priming |
| Pumps in series – head adds | Pumps in parallel – discharge adds |
| Viscosity of liquid ↓ with T | Viscosity of gas ↑ with T |
| Droplet Δp = 4σ/d | Soap bubble Δp = 8σ/d |
| Streamline/pathline differ (unsteady) | Same (steady) |
| Stream function – any 2D incompressible flow | Velocity potential – only irrotational flow |
| Up milling – chip thickness 0 → max | Down milling – chip thickness max → 0 |
| Jig – guides tool | Fixture – holds work only |
| Hole basis – H (preferred) | Shaft basis – h |
| PERT – probabilistic, event-oriented | CPM – deterministic, activity-oriented |
| p chart – fraction defective | c chart – number of defects |
| Producer's risk α – type I error | Consumer's risk β – type II error |
| SI knocking – end of combustion | CI knocking – beginning of combustion |
| Octane – iso-octane & n-heptane | Cetane – cetane & α-methyl naphthalene |
| Quantity governing (SI) | Quality governing (CI) |
| Fire-tube – Cochran, Lancashire, Locomotive | Water-tube – B&W, La Mont, Benson |
| Mountings – safety (safety valve, fusible plug) | Accessories – efficiency (economiser, air preheater) |
| Dropwise condensation – higher h | Filmwise – used for design |
| Parallel flow HX | Counter flow HX – higher LMTD, more effective |
| Brass – Cu + Zn | Bronze – Cu + Sn |
| Thermoplastic – recyclable (PVC, PE) | Thermoset – permanent (Bakelite, epoxy) |
