# Solved Concept Numericals – Set 3 (Concept Twisters)
These are "ratio and proportion" twisters. SSC likes them because they test understanding, not plug-in. Each one shows the shortcut a topper uses.

## 16C.1 Ratio-Type Twisters – SOM & TOM
### Problem 121 – Doubling diameter of a rod
If a rod's diameter is doubled with load and length unchanged, what happens to stress and elongation?
F: σ ∝ 1/d² → σ becomes 1/4;  δ ∝ 1/d² → δ becomes 1/4
### Problem 122 – Beam depth doubled
How does the bending strength of a rectangular beam change if its depth is doubled? Its stiffness?
F: Z = bd²/6 → strength ×4;  I = bd³/12 → stiffness ×8, deflection becomes 1/8
### Problem 123 – Span doubled
For a SS beam with UDL (same w), what happens to max BM and max deflection if the span doubles?
F: M ∝ L² → ×4;  δ ∝ L⁴ → ×16
### Problem 124 – Shaft diameter doubled
How do torque capacity and torsional stiffness change if the shaft diameter doubles?
F: T ∝ d³ → ×8;  GJ/L ∝ d⁴ → ×16
### Problem 125 – Hollow vs solid shaft (same outer diameter)
A hollow shaft has d/D = 0.5. Compare its strength and weight with a solid shaft of the same D.
F: Strength ratio = 1 − (d/D)⁴ = 1 − 0.0625 = 0.9375 (only 6.25% less)
F: Weight ratio = 1 − (d/D)² = 0.75 (25% lighter) → this is why hollow shafts are preferred
### Problem 126 – Column length doubled
What happens to the Euler load if the length of a column doubles?
F: P ∝ 1/L² → becomes 1/4
### Problem 127 – Column diameter doubled
What happens to the Euler load if a circular column's diameter doubles?
F: P ∝ I ∝ d⁴ → ×16
### Problem 128 – Spring wire diameter doubled
For a close-coiled spring with D and n fixed, what happens to stiffness if the wire diameter doubles?
F: k ∝ d⁴ → ×16
### Problem 129 – Spring coils halved
What happens to stiffness if the number of coils is halved?
F: k ∝ 1/n → ×2
### Problem 130 – Natural frequency with mass quadrupled
How does the natural frequency change if the mass becomes 4 times?
F: ωn ∝ 1/√m → becomes 1/2
### Problem 131 – Governor speed doubled
How does the height of a Watt governor change if speed doubles?
F: h ∝ 1/N² → becomes 1/4
### Problem 132 – Flywheel speed doubled
For the same Cs and I, how does ΔE change if the speed doubles?
F: ΔE ∝ ω² → ×4  (so a flywheel at higher speed can be lighter: I ∝ 1/ω²)
### Problem 133 – Centrifugal tension
How does centrifugal tension change if belt speed doubles?
F: Tc = mv² → ×4
### Problem 134 – Bearing load reduced
If the load on a roller bearing halves, how does its life change?
F: L ∝ (1/P)^(10/3) → ×2^(3.33) ≈ ×10

## 16C.2 Ratio-Type Twisters – Thermal & HT
### Problem 135 – Carnot with both temperatures raised
A Carnot engine works between 600 K and 300 K. Both temperatures rise by 100 K. New efficiency?
F: Old: 1 − 300/600 = 50%;  New: 1 − 400/700 = 42.9% → efficiency falls
TIP: Lowering the sink temperature by ΔT raises η more than raising the source temperature by the same ΔT, because ∂η/∂T₂ = −1/T₁ is larger in magnitude than ∂η/∂T₁ = T₂/T₁².
### Problem 136 – Refrigerator COP with smaller temperature lift
A Carnot refrigerator works between 250 K and 300 K. The condenser temperature drops to 275 K. New COP?
F: Old COP = 250/50 = 5;  New = 250/25 = 10 (doubles)
### Problem 137 – Otto efficiency for r = 4 and 16
Compare Otto efficiencies (γ = 1.4) for r = 4 and r = 16.
F: r = 4: 1 − 1/4^0.4 = 1 − 1/1.741 = 42.6%;  r = 16: 1 − 1/3.031 = 67%
### Problem 138 – Ideal gas heated at constant pressure
Air is heated at constant pressure from 300 K to 600 K. How do volume and density change?
F: V ∝ T → doubles;  ρ ∝ 1/T → halves
### Problem 139 – Heat flow when thickness doubles
Steady conduction through a slab: how does Q change if thickness doubles and k halves?
F: Q ∝ k/L → (1/2)/(2) = 1/4
### Problem 140 – Radiation when T doubles
How does a black body's emissive power change when absolute temperature doubles?
F: E ∝ T⁴ → ×16;  λ_max halves (Wien)
### Problem 141 – Fin with longer length
For an infinitely long fin, does doubling the length change heat transfer?
F: No – Q = √(hPkA)θ₀ is independent of length for a long fin (tanh(mL) → 1)
### Problem 142 – Critical radius check
A wire of 2 mm radius is covered with insulation (k = 0.2 W/mK, h = 20 W/m²K). Will adding insulation increase heat loss?
F: r_c = k/h = 0.01 m = 10 mm > 2 mm → yes, heat loss increases until the outer radius reaches 10 mm
### Problem 143 – Rankine with lower condenser pressure
State qualitatively what happens to turbine work, η, exhaust dryness and heat rejected when condenser pressure is lowered.
F: W_T ↑, η ↑, dryness ↓ (more moisture), heat rejected per kg ↑ only slightly (temperature of rejection ↓)
### Problem 144 – Nozzle exit velocity with double enthalpy drop
If the enthalpy drop doubles, what is the new velocity?
F: V ∝ √Δh → ×√2 = ×1.414

## 16C.3 Ratio-Type Twisters – Fluids & Machines
### Problem 145 – Pipe diameter doubled (same Q, turbulent, Darcy)
For the same discharge and f, how does head loss change if pipe diameter doubles?
F: h_f ∝ Q²/D⁵ → becomes 1/32
### Problem 146 – Laminar flow, diameter doubled (same Q)
How does the pressure drop in laminar flow change if diameter doubles at the same discharge?
F: Δp ∝ Q/D⁴ (Hagen–Poiseuille) → becomes 1/16
### Problem 147 – Capillary tube diameter halved
What happens to capillary rise when tube diameter halves?
F: h ∝ 1/d → doubles
### Problem 148 – Notch head doubled
How does discharge change when head doubles over a rectangular weir and over a V-notch?
F: Rectangular: Q ∝ H^1.5 → ×2.83;  V-notch: Q ∝ H^2.5 → ×5.66
### Problem 149 – Pump speed increased by 20%
How do Q, H and P change if pump speed increases by 20%?
F: Q ×1.2;  H ×1.44;  P ×1.728
### Problem 150 – Turbine head doubled (same turbine)
For the same turbine, how do speed, discharge and power change if head doubles (unit quantities constant)?
F: N ∝ √H → ×1.414;  Q ∝ √H → ×1.414;  P ∝ H^1.5 → ×2.83
### Problem 151 – Jet velocity doubled
How does the force of a jet on a fixed plate change if jet velocity doubles?
F: F = ρaV² → ×4
### Problem 152 – Reynolds model of a pipe
A 1:10 model of a pipe uses the same fluid. Find the velocity ratio Vm/Vp for Reynolds similarity.
F: VmLm = VpLp → Vm/Vp = 10 (model runs 10 times faster)
### Problem 153 – Froude model time scale
For a 1:100 spillway model, a flood rises in 1 hour in the prototype. How long does it take in the model?
F: Tr = √Lr → Tm = 60/10 = 6 min
### Problem 154 – Drag at double speed
For constant C_D, how does drag change when speed doubles? Power?
F: Drag ∝ V² → ×4;  power = D·V ∝ V³ → ×8

## 16C.4 Ratio-Type Twisters – Production & IE
### Problem 155 – Taylor equation with n = 0.5
How does tool life change if cutting speed doubles (n = 0.5)?
F: T ∝ V^(−1/n) = V^(−2) → becomes 1/4
### Problem 156 – Surface roughness with feed doubled
How does peak-to-valley roughness change if feed doubles?
F: h = f²/(8r) → ×4
### Problem 157 – Nose radius doubled
How does roughness change if nose radius doubles?
F: h ∝ 1/r → halves
### Problem 158 – Casting size doubled
How does solidification time change if all dimensions of a casting double?
F: V/A doubles → t ∝ (V/A)² → ×4
### Problem 159 – Blanking force for double thickness
How does blanking force change if sheet thickness doubles?
F: F = Ltτ → doubles
### Problem 160 – EOQ with demand ×4
How does EOQ change if annual demand becomes 4 times?
F: Q* ∝ √D → doubles
### Problem 161 – EOQ with holding cost ×4
How does EOQ change if holding cost becomes 4 times?
F: Q* ∝ 1/√Ch → halves
### Problem 162 – Reliability of a 2-out-of-2 vs 1-out-of-2 system
Two units each have R = 0.8. Compare series and parallel.
F: Series: 0.64;  Parallel: 1 − 0.04 = 0.96
### Problem 163 – Work sampling accuracy
How many times the observations are needed if the required accuracy is halved (made twice as tight)?
F: N ∝ 1/S² → ×4
### Problem 164 – Rolling – roll radius doubled
How does maximum draft change if roll radius doubles (same μ)?
F: Δh_max = μ²R → doubles
### Problem 165 – Welding heat input
How does heat input per unit length change if travel speed doubles at the same V and I?
F: H = ηVI/v → halves

## 16C.5 Multi-Step Numericals (Paper-II Style)
### Problem 166 – Beam design
A simply supported timber beam of 4 m span carries 6 kN/m UDL. σ_allow = 10 MPa and d = 2b. Find b and d.
F: M = wL²/8 = 6 × 16/8 = 12 kN·m = 12 × 10⁶ N·mm
F: Z = M/σ = 1.2 × 10⁶ mm³;  bd²/6 = b(2b)²/6 = 2b³/3 = 1.2 × 10⁶ → b³ = 1.8 × 10⁶ → b = 121.6 mm, d = 243 mm
### Problem 167 – Shaft under bending and torsion
A shaft carries M = 3 kN·m and T = 4 kN·m. τ_allow = 50 MPa. Find d by maximum shear stress theory.
F: Te = √(3² + 4²) = 5 kN·m = 5 × 10⁶ N·mm
F: d³ = 16Te/(πτ) = 16 × 5 × 10⁶/(π × 50) = 5.093 × 10⁵ → d = 79.9 ≈ 80 mm
### Problem 168 – Engine test
A 4-stroke, 4-cylinder petrol engine (bore 80 mm, stroke 100 mm) at 3000 rpm has BP = 30 kW and fuel consumption 9 kg/h (CV 44 MJ/kg). Find bsfc and brake thermal efficiency.
F: bsfc = 9/30 = 0.3 kg/kWh
F: Heat input = 9 × 44000/3600 = 110 kW;  ηbth = 30/110 = 27.3%
### Problem 169 – Swept volume and CR
A cylinder has bore 100 mm, stroke 120 mm and clearance volume 120 cc. Find swept volume and compression ratio.
F: Vs = (π/4) × 10² × 12 = 942.5 cc;  r = (942.5 + 120)/120 = 8.85
### Problem 170 – Heat exchanger outlet
Hot oil (C_h = 2000 W/K) enters at 150 °C and cold water (C_c = 4000 W/K) at 30 °C, ε = 0.6. Find Q and outlet temperatures.
F: C_min = 2000;  Q_max = 2000 × 120 = 240 kW;  Q = 0.6 × 240 = 144 kW
F: T_h,out = 150 − 144000/2000 = 78 °C;  T_c,out = 30 + 144000/4000 = 66 °C
### Problem 171 – Pipe network (series)
Two pipes in series: 1000 m of 0.3 m diameter and 500 m of 0.2 m diameter. Find the equivalent length of 0.3 m pipe (same f).
F: Le = L₁ + L₂(D₁/D₂)⁵ = 1000 + 500 × (1.5)⁵ = 1000 + 500 × 7.594 = 4797 m
### Problem 172 – Turbine selection
A site has 30 m head and 20 m³/s flow. η = 0.9 and N = 150 rpm. Find power and Ns, and select the turbine.
F: P = 0.9 × 9810 × 20 × 30 = 5297 kW;  Ns = 150√5297/30^1.25 = 150 × 72.8/70.2 = 155.5
F: Ns ≈ 155 → Francis turbine (medium specific speed)
### Problem 173 – Pump power
A pump lifts 50 L/s through a total head of 30 m with overall efficiency 75%. Find the input power.
F: P = ρgQH/η = 9810 × 0.05 × 30/0.75 = 19.6 kW
### Problem 174 – Merchant force analysis
Orthogonal cutting: Fc = 1200 N, Ft = 500 N, α = 10°, φ = 30°. Find friction force, normal force and μ.
F: F = Fc sinα + Ft cosα = 208.4 + 492.4 = 700.8 N
F: N = Fc cosα − Ft sinα = 1181.8 − 86.8 = 1095 N;  μ = 0.64
F: Fs = Fc cosφ − Ft sinφ = 1039 − 250 = 789 N
### Problem 175 – EOQ with total cost
D = 12000/yr, Co = Rs 150, unit cost Rs 20, carrying 15% per year. Find EOQ and total inventory cost (excluding purchase).
F: Ch = 0.15 × 20 = 3;  Q* = √(2 × 12000 × 150/3) = √1.2 × 10⁶ = 1095 units
F: TC = √(2 × 12000 × 150 × 3) = Rs 3286 per year
### Problem 176 – CPM network
Activities (duration): A(3), B(4) after A, C(2) after A, D(5) after B and C, E(1) after C. Find the critical path and duration.
Paths: A–B–D = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12;  A–C–D = 3 + 2 + 5 = 10;  A–C–E = 6.
F: Critical path A–B–D, duration 12 days. Total float of C = LF − EF = 7 − 5 = 2 days
