# Solved Concept Numericals – Set 2
A second set with slightly more steps, the kind that appears as 2–3 mark numericals in Paper-II and as tricky Paper-I questions.

## 16B.1 Mechanics & SOM
### Problem 61 – Ladder friction
A uniform 5 m ladder weighing W rests against a smooth wall with its foot 3 m from the wall on rough ground. Find the minimum μ at the floor for equilibrium.
Height reached = 4 m. Wall reaction R_w is horizontal; floor gives N = W and friction F = R_w. Take moments about the foot: R_w × 4 = W × 1.5 → R_w = 0.375W.
F: μ_min = F/N = 0.375
### Problem 62 – Body on an incline
A 100 N block sits on a 30° incline with μ = 0.2. Find the force parallel to the plane needed to push it up.
F: P = W(sinθ + μcosθ) = 100(0.5 + 0.2 × 0.866) = 100 × 0.673 = 67.3 N
### Problem 63 – Composite centroid
An L-section is made of two rectangles: 100 × 20 (horizontal flange at the bottom) and 20 × 80 (vertical, sitting on the flange at the left). Find ȳ from the bottom.
F: A₁ = 2000, y₁ = 10;  A₂ = 1600, y₂ = 20 + 40 = 60
F: ȳ = (2000 × 10 + 1600 × 60)/(3600) = (20000 + 96000)/3600 = 32.2 mm
### Problem 64 – Parallel axis
Find the MOI of a 100 × 200 mm rectangle about an axis 50 mm below its base.
F: I_G = 100 × 200³/12 = 6.667 × 10⁷;  h = 100 + 50 = 150 mm
F: I = I_G + Ah² = 6.667 × 10⁷ + 20000 × 22500 = 6.667 × 10⁷ + 4.5 × 10⁸ = 5.167 × 10⁸ mm⁴
### Problem 65 – Composite bar
A steel rod (A = 500 mm², E = 200 GPa) and a copper tube (A = 1000 mm², E = 100 GPa) of equal length share a load of 150 kN. Find stresses.
Equal strain: σs/200 = σc/100 → σs = 2σc. Load: 500σs + 1000σc = 150000 → 1000σc + 1000σc = 150000.
F: σc = 75 MPa;  σs = 150 MPa
### Problem 66 – Strain energy
Find the strain energy in a bar 1 m long, area 100 mm², under 20 kN (E = 200 GPa).
F: U = P²L/(2AE) = (20000)² × 1000 / (2 × 100 × 200000) = 4 × 10¹¹ / 4 × 10⁷ = 10,000 N·mm = 10 J
### Problem 67 – Overhanging beam contraflexure
A beam of span 6 m (supports at A and B) overhangs 2 m beyond B and carries UDL 10 kN/m over the whole 8 m. Find the reactions and the point of contraflexure.
Moments about A: R_B × 6 = 10 × 8 × 4 → R_B = 53.33 kN;  R_A = 80 − 53.33 = 26.67 kN.
Between A and B at x from A: M = 26.67x − 5x². M = 0 at x = 5.33 m (other than A).
F: Point of contraflexure 5.33 m from A (0.67 m left of B)
### Problem 68 – Hollow shaft torque
A hollow shaft (D = 100 mm, d = 60 mm) has allowable τ = 80 MPa. Find the torque capacity.
F: T = (π/16)τ(D⁴ − d⁴)/D = (π/16) × 80 × (10⁸ − 1.296 × 10⁷)/100 = 15.708 × 8.704 × 10⁵ ≈ 1.367 × 10⁷ N·mm = 13.67 kN·m
### Problem 69 – Angle of twist
A solid shaft of 50 mm diameter, 1 m long, carries T = 1 kN·m (G = 80 GPa). Find the twist.
F: J = π × 50⁴/32 = 6.136 × 10⁵ mm⁴;  θ = TL/(GJ) = 10⁶ × 1000/(80000 × 6.136 × 10⁵) = 0.0204 rad = 1.17°
### Problem 70 – Stresses on an oblique plane
A bar under σ = 100 MPa uniaxial tension. Find σn and τ on a plane at 30° to the cross-section.
F: σn = σcos²θ = 100 × 0.75 = 75 MPa;  τ = (σ/2) sin2θ = 50 × 0.866 = 43.3 MPa
### Problem 71 – Thin cylinder volume change
A thin cylinder: d = 1 m, t = 10 mm, p = 2 MPa, E = 200 GPa, μ = 0.3. Find the volumetric strain.
F: εv = (pd/4tE)(5 − 4μ) = (2 × 1000/(4 × 10 × 200000))(5 − 1.2) = 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ × 3.8 = 9.5 × 10⁻⁴
### Problem 72 – Rankine column
A short column has σc = 320 MPa, A = 4000 mm², λ = 100, a = 1/7500. Find the Rankine load.
F: P = σcA/(1 + aλ²) = 320 × 4000/(1 + 10000/7500) = 1.28 × 10⁶/2.333 = 548.6 kN

## 16B.2 TOM & Machine Design
### Problem 73 – Quick return ratio
In a crank & slotted lever QRM, crank length is 100 mm and the distance between centres is 200 mm. Find the time ratio.
F: cos(α/2) = r/c = 0.5 → α/2 = 60° → return angle α = 120°
F: Ratio = (360 − 120)/120 = 2
### Problem 74 – Open belt length
Pulleys of 400 mm and 200 mm diameter are 1 m apart. Find the open belt length.
F: L = π(r₁ + r₂) + 2x + (r₁ − r₂)²/x = π(300) + 2000 + 100²/1000 = 942.5 + 2000 + 10 = 2952.5 mm
### Problem 75 – Belt power
In a belt drive T₁ = 2000 N, T₂ = 800 N, v = 15 m/s. Find power.
F: P = (T₁ − T₂)v = 1200 × 15 = 18 kW
### Problem 76 – Epicyclic train
In an epicyclic train the sun has 40 T and the ring 100 T. The ring is fixed and the sun turns at 700 rpm. Find arm speed.
Planet teeth = (100 − 40)/2 = 30. Tabular method: arm fixed → sun +1, ring −40/100. Add y: ring = y − 0.4x = 0; sun = x + y = 700 → x = 500, y = 200.
F: Arm speed N_arm = N_sun × T_s/(T_s + T_r) = 700 × 40/140 = 200 rpm (same direction)
### Problem 77 – Clutch torque
A single-plate clutch (both sides effective) has r₁ = 150 mm, r₂ = 100 mm, W = 4 kN, μ = 0.3. Find torque (uniform wear).
F: T = n μ W (r₁ + r₂)/2 = 2 × 0.3 × 4000 × 125 = 300,000 N·mm = 300 N·m
### Problem 78 – Damped vibration
A system has m = 10 kg, k = 4000 N/m, c = 100 N·s/m. Find ζ and ωd.
F: ωn = √(4000/10) = 20 rad/s;  c_c = 2mωn = 400;  ζ = 0.25;  ωd = 20√(1 − 0.0625) = 19.36 rad/s
### Problem 79 – Gear contact
Two 20° gears of module 5 mm have 20 and 40 teeth. Find centre distance and pitch circle diameters.
F: d₁ = 100 mm, d₂ = 200 mm;  centre distance = (100 + 200)/2 = 150 mm;  circular pitch = πm = 15.7 mm
### Problem 80 – Riveted joint efficiency
Plate t = 10 mm, rivet d = 20 mm, pitch p = 60 mm, single-riveted lap joint. σt = 100, τ = 80, σc = 160 MPa. Find efficiency.
F: Pt = (60 − 20) × 10 × 100 = 40 kN;  Ps = (π/4)(400) × 80 = 25.1 kN;  Pc = 20 × 10 × 160 = 32 kN
F: Solid plate strength = 60 × 10 × 100 = 60 kN;  η = 25.1/60 = 41.9% (governed by shear)
### Problem 81 – Fillet weld length
A plate is joined by a double parallel fillet weld of size 10 mm carrying 100 kN. τ = 80 MPa. Find the weld length (each side).
F: P = 2 × 0.707 × s × l × τ → l = 100000/(2 × 0.707 × 10 × 80) = 88.4 mm (+ 12.5 mm for start/stop ≈ 100 mm)
### Problem 82 – Soderberg design
A steel bar has σm = 100 MPa and σa = 60 MPa. Syt = 400 MPa, Se = 200 MPa. Find FOS (Soderberg).
F: 1/n = σa/Se + σm/Syt = 0.3 + 0.25 = 0.55 → n = 1.82
### Problem 83 – Bearing selection
A ball bearing must carry 2 kN for 100 million revolutions. Find the required dynamic capacity C.
F: C = P × L^(1/3) = 2 × 100^(1/3) = 2 × 4.64 = 9.28 kN

## 16B.3 Thermal & HT
### Problem 84 – Polytropic work
0.1 m³ of air at 1 bar is compressed polytropically (n = 1.3) to 8 bar. Find V₂ and work.
F: V₂ = 0.1 × (1/8)^(1/1.3) = 0.1 × 0.2016 = 0.0202 m³
F: W = (p₂V₂ − p₁V₁)/(n − 1) = (800 × 0.0202 − 100 × 0.1)/0.3 = (16.13 − 10)/0.3 = 20.4 kJ (done on gas)
### Problem 85 – Adiabatic temperature rise
Air at 300 K is compressed isentropically through a pressure ratio of 8. Find T₂.
F: T₂ = 300 × 8^(0.4/1.4) = 300 × 8^0.2857 = 300 × 1.811 = 543 K
### Problem 86 – Diesel cycle
Diesel cycle with r = 16 and ρ = 2 (γ = 1.4). Find efficiency.
F: r^0.4 = 3.031;  (ρ^γ − 1)/(γ(ρ − 1)) = (2.639 − 1)/1.4 = 1.171
F: η = 1 − 1.171/3.031 = 1 − 0.386 = 61.4%
### Problem 87 – Brayton cycle
Find the efficiency of a Brayton cycle with pressure ratio 10.
F: η = 1 − 1/10^(0.2857) = 1 − 1/1.931 = 48.2%
### Problem 88 – Steam quality
Steam at 10 bar has h = 2500 kJ/kg. At 10 bar, hf = 762.6, hfg = 2013.6 kJ/kg. Find the dryness fraction.
F: x = (2500 − 762.6)/2013.6 = 0.863
### Problem 89 – Rankine cycle (simplified)
Turbine inlet h₃ = 3200, exit h₄ = 2200, pump exit h₂ = 200, condensate h₁ = 192 (kJ/kg). Find η and SSC.
F: W_T = 1000;  W_P = 8;  Q₁ = 3200 − 200 = 3000;  η = 992/3000 = 33.1%;  SSC = 3600/992 = 3.63 kg/kWh
### Problem 90 – Volumetric efficiency of compressor
Clearance ratio 0.05, pressure ratio 7, n = 1.3. Find ηv.
F: ηv = 1 + C − C(7)^(1/1.3) = 1.05 − 0.05 × 4.46 = 1.05 − 0.223 = 82.7%
### Problem 91 – VCR from enthalpies
h₁ = 1450, h₂ = 1650, h₃ = h₄ = 350 kJ/kg (ammonia). Find COP and heat rejected per kg.
F: RE = 1100;  W = 200;  COP = 5.5;  Q_cond = 1300 kJ/kg
### Problem 92 – Psychrometry
Air at 1.013 bar has a partial pressure of vapour of 0.02 bar. Find specific humidity.
F: ω = 0.622 × 0.02/(1.013 − 0.02) = 0.01244/0.993 = 0.01253 kg/kg dry air
### Problem 93 – Composite wall
A brick wall (L = 0.2 m, k = 0.8) has plaster (L = 0.02 m, k = 0.4) on one side. h_in = 10, h_out = 20 W/m²K, ΔT = 30 °C. Find U and q.
F: 1/U = 0.1 + 0.25 + 0.05 + 0.05 = 0.45 → U = 2.22 W/m²K;  q = 2.22 × 30 = 66.7 W/m²
### Problem 94 – Lumped cooling
A steel ball (ρ = 7800, c = 460, r = 5 mm, k = 40) is cooled with h = 100 W/m²K. Check lumped validity and find the time constant.
F: Lc = r/3 = 1.667 mm;  Bi = 100 × 0.001667/40 = 0.0042 < 0.1 → valid
F: τ = ρcLc/h = 7800 × 460 × 0.001667/100 = 59.8 s
### Problem 95 – Radiation between plates
Two large parallel plates at 800 K and 400 K, both ε = 0.8. Find net radiation per m².
F: q = σ(T₁⁴ − T₂⁴)/(2/ε − 1) = 5.67 × 10⁻⁸ × (4.096 × 10¹¹ − 2.56 × 10¹⁰)/1.5 = 5.67 × 10⁻⁸ × 3.84 × 10¹¹/1.5 = 14.5 kW/m²
### Problem 96 – Heat exchanger NTU
A condenser has UA = 2000 W/K and cooling water C = 1000 W/K. Find effectiveness.
F: NTU = 2;  ε = 1 − e^(−2) = 0.865

## 16B.4 Fluids, Machines, Production
### Problem 97 – Manometer
A U-tube mercury manometer connected to a water pipe shows a 0.2 m deflection (other limb open, centre of pipe 0.1 m above the mercury level in the pipe-side limb). Find pipe pressure.
F: p + 9810 × 0.1 = 13.6 × 9810 × 0.2 → p = 26683 − 981 = 25.7 kPa (gauge)
### Problem 98 – Venturimeter
A 200 × 100 mm venturimeter shows a 0.5 m water-head difference. Cd = 0.98. Find Q.
F: A₁ = 0.03142, A₂ = 0.007854;  Q = Cd A₁A₂√(2gh)/√(A₁² − A₂²)
F: = 0.98 × 2.467 × 10⁻⁴ × 3.132 / 0.03042 = 0.0249 m³/s ≈ 25 L/s
### Problem 99 – Buoyancy
A wooden block (S = 0.6) of 1 m × 0.5 m × 0.4 m (height) floats in water. Find the depth of immersion.
F: 0.6 × 0.4 = 1 × h → h = 0.24 m
### Problem 100 – Metacentric height
For the block above floating with the 0.4 m side vertical, find GM (rolling about the long axis).
F: I = 1 × 0.5³/12 = 0.01042 m⁴;  V = 1 × 0.5 × 0.24 = 0.12 m³;  BM = 0.0868 m
F: BG = 0.2 − 0.12 = 0.08 m;  GM = 0.0868 − 0.08 = 0.0068 m > 0 → stable (barely)
### Problem 101 – Water hammer
Water flows at 2 m/s in a rigid pipe and the valve closes suddenly (C = 1400 m/s). Find the pressure rise.
F: Δp = ρVC = 1000 × 2 × 1400 = 2.8 MPa
### Problem 102 – Froude model
A 1:25 ship model is tested. The prototype speed is 10 m/s. Find model speed and the force scale.
F: Vm = Vp/√25 = 2 m/s;  force ratio = Lr³ = 15625
### Problem 103 – Pelton power
Q = 0.5 m³/s, H = 300 m, ηo = 0.85. Find the power.
F: P = ηo ρgQH = 0.85 × 9810 × 0.5 × 300 = 1.25 MW
### Problem 104 – Centrifugal pump head
Impeller outlet D₂ = 0.4 m, N = 1450 rpm, Vw₂ = 25 m/s, radial entry. Find Euler head and manometric efficiency if Hm = 50 m.
F: u₂ = π × 0.4 × 1450/60 = 30.4 m/s;  H_e = Vw₂u₂/g = 25 × 30.4/9.81 = 77.4 m;  ηmano = 50/77.4 = 64.6%
### Problem 105 – Riser design
A casting is a 100 × 100 × 100 mm cube. A cylindrical riser with H = D must take 1.25 times the solidification time of the casting. Find D.
F: (V/A) cube = 100/6 = 16.67 mm;  cylinder H = D: V/A = D/6
F: t ∝ (V/A)² → (D/6)² = 1.25 × 16.67² → D/6 = 18.63 → D = 111.8 mm
### Problem 106 – Shear angle from chip ratio
In orthogonal cutting t = 0.5 mm, t_c = 1.0 mm, α = 10°. Find φ.
F: r = 0.5;  tanφ = 0.5cos10°/(1 − 0.5sin10°) = 0.4924/0.9132 = 0.539 → φ = 28.3°
### Problem 107 – Cutting power
Fc = 1000 N, V = 120 m/min. Find the cutting power.
F: P = 1000 × 2 = 2 kW
### Problem 108 – Drilling time
Drill a 20 mm hole 40 mm deep at 500 rpm and 0.2 mm/rev feed. Find time.
F: t = (L + 0.3D)/(fN) = (40 + 6)/(0.2 × 500) = 0.46 min
### Problem 109 – Deep drawing blank
Find the blank diameter for a cup of d = 50 mm, h = 30 mm (sharp corners).
F: D = √(d² + 4dh) = √(2500 + 6000) = 92.2 mm;  draw ratio 1.84 < 2 → single draw OK
### Problem 110 – Bending of a sheet
Find the bend allowance for a 90° bend with R = 5 mm, t = 2 mm, k = 0.33.
F: BA = α(R + kt) = (π/2)(5 + 0.66) = 8.89 mm
### Problem 111 – Tolerance grade
Find IT7 for a diameter in the 30–50 mm step.
F: D = √(30 × 50) = 38.73;  i = 0.45 × 3.383 + 0.0387 = 1.561 μm;  IT7 = 16i = 25 μm
### Problem 112 – Sine bar
A 200 mm sine bar is used to set 30°. Find the slip gauge height.
F: h = L sinθ = 200 × 0.5 = 100 mm
### Problem 113 – Johnson's rule
Jobs (M1, M2): A(5,2), B(1,6), C(9,7), D(3,8), E(10,4). Find the sequence.
Smallest times: B (1 on M1) → first; A (2 on M2) → last; D (3 on M1) → second; E (4 on M2) → second last; C remains.
F: Sequence: B – D – C – E – A
### Problem 114 – Exponential smoothing
Forecast for May = 100, actual May = 120, α = 0.2. Find the June forecast.
F: F_June = 100 + 0.2(120 − 100) = 104
### Problem 115 – M/M/1 queue
λ = 8/h, μ = 10/h. Find Ls, Lq, Ws.
F: Ls = 8/2 = 4;  Lq = 64/(10 × 2) = 3.2;  Ws = 1/2 h = 30 min
### Problem 116 – Work sampling observations
An activity is expected to occupy 25% of the time. How many observations are needed for ±5% relative accuracy at 95% confidence?
F: N = 4(1 − p)/(S²p) = 4 × 0.75/(0.0025 × 0.25) = 4800
### Problem 117 – p-chart limits
p̄ = 0.04, n = 100. Find the control limits.
F: σ = √(0.04 × 0.96/100) = 0.0196;  UCL = 0.04 + 0.0588 = 0.0988;  LCL = 0.04 − 0.0588 < 0 → 0
### Problem 118 – Process capability
USL = 10.05, LSL = 9.95 mm, σ = 0.01 mm. Find Cp.
F: Cp = 0.1/(6 × 0.01) = 1.67 → capable
### Problem 119 – Probability of project completion
Critical path te = 40 days, σ = 4 days. Find the probability of finishing in 44 days.
F: Z = (44 − 40)/4 = 1 → P = 84.1%
### Problem 120 – Straight-line depreciation
A machine costs Rs 1,00,000, has salvage Rs 10,000 and a 9-year life. Find annual depreciation.
F: D = (100000 − 10000)/9 = Rs 10,000 per year
