# SSC Eduquity Era (2025–26) – GA Analysis & Important Questions

`SSC_Eduquity_Era_GA_2025-26_Analysis_and_Important_Questions.pdf` (99 pages, 725 MCQs): analysis of the
SSC exams held under the new vendor Eduquity (Selection Post XIII, CGL 2025, CHSL 2025, MTS 2025, GD 2026,
CGL 2026) – pattern, difficulty and GS topic trends from coaching-site analyses; 42 reported CGL 2026
questions as MCQs; 683 new important-question MCQs on the trend topics; three 25-question sets. Every MCQ has
the answer and an explanation. Important questions are original practice MCQs, not previous-year questions.
