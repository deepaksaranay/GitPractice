# Reported Questions (CGL 2026, MCQ Form)

## 2.1 Polity
RM: Which Article of the Constitution states that 'India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States'? [1 Oct · Shift 1 · PW] || Article 1 || Article 2 || Article 3 || Article 4 || a || Article 1 opens Part I. 'Union of States' means the federation is not the result of an agreement among states and no state can secede. Article 2 admits new states from outside; Article 3 forms new states or alters boundaries within India.
RM: The right to move the Supreme Court for enforcement of Fundamental Rights is given by which Article? [1 Oct · Shift 1 · PW; 5 Oct · Prepp] || Article 19 || Article 21 || Article 32 || Article 226 || c || Article 32 is itself a Fundamental Right; Ambedkar called it the 'heart and soul' of the Constitution. The SC issues five writs under it. High Courts issue writs under Article 226, which also covers legal rights.
RM: Article 131A, which gave the Supreme Court exclusive jurisdiction over the validity of central laws, was inserted by which amendment? [1 Oct · Shift 1 · Examshortcut, PW] || 24th Amendment || 42nd Amendment || 44th Amendment || 52nd Amendment || b || The 42nd Amendment (1976) inserted Articles 131A, 144A and 226A to curb High Courts' review of central laws. The 43rd Amendment (1977) omitted all three. Article 131 (Centre–state disputes) remains.
RM: Article 243 of the Constitution deals with: [1 Oct · Shift 1 · Examshortcut, PW] || Definitions for Panchayats (Part IX) || Municipalities || Co-operative societies || Scheduled Areas || a || The 73rd Amendment (1992) added Part IX (Articles 243–243-O) and the Eleventh Schedule (29 subjects). Article 243A – Gram Sabha; 243D – reservation (≥ 1/3 for women); 243K – State Election Commission.
RM: The 97th Constitutional Amendment (2011) is related to: [1 Oct · Shift 1 · Examshortcut] || Anti-defection || Co-operative societies || GST || Right to education || b || It added 'co-operative societies' to Article 19(1)(c), inserted Article 43B (DPSP) and Part IX-B (Articles 243ZH–243ZT). Anti-defection = 52nd; GST = 101st; Right to Education = 86th.
RM: Under which Article can the Rajya Sabha, by a two-thirds resolution, enable Parliament to create new All India Services? [5 Oct · Prepp] || Article 249 || Article 312 || Article 315 || Article 368 || b || Article 312 – new All India Services (including an All India Judicial Service). Article 249 – Rajya Sabha resolution to let Parliament legislate on a State List subject. Article 315 – Public Service Commissions.
RM: Which Article provides for a financial emergency? [1 Oct · Shift 1 · PW] || Article 352 || Article 356 || Article 360 || Article 365 || c || Article 360 – financial emergency; never declared so far. Article 352 – national emergency (1962, 1971, 1975). Article 356 – President's Rule. Article 365 – failure to comply with Union directions.
RM: Under the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, a 'zero FIR' means an FIR that: [1 Oct · Shift 1 · Examshortcut, PW] || Has zero accused named || Can be registered at any police station regardless of jurisdiction || Is filed only online || Is withdrawn within 24 hours || b || Section 173 BNSS lets any police station register information on a cognisable offence and transfer it to the station with jurisdiction. BNS, BNSS and BSA replaced the IPC, CrPC and Evidence Act on 1 July 2024.
RM: The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 replaced which law? [1 Oct · Shift 1 · Examshortcut] || Code of Criminal Procedure || Indian Evidence Act || Indian Penal Code || Indian Police Act || c || BNS replaced the IPC (1860); BNSS replaced the CrPC (1973); Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam replaced the Indian Evidence Act (1872). All came into force on 1 July 2024.
