# Important MCQs – General Science

## 8.1 Physics

M: The unit of radioactivity is: || Becquerel || Gray || Sievert || Tesla || a || Curie = 3.7 × 10¹⁰ Bq.
M: A parsec is a unit of: || Time || Distance || Mass || Energy || b || About 3.26 light years.
M: Normal human body temperature on the Kelvin scale is about: || 273 K || 300 K || 310 K || 373 K || c || 37 °C + 273.
M: Newton's first law of motion is also called the law of: || Acceleration || Inertia || Action and reaction || Gravitation || b || A bus passenger jerks forward when the bus stops suddenly.
M: Catching a cricket ball by moving the hands backward reduces the force because it: || Reduces momentum || Increases the time of impact || Increases mass || Reduces velocity to zero instantly || b || Impulse = force × time.
M: The weight of a body on the Moon is about: || Same as on Earth || One-sixth of that on Earth || Six times that on Earth || Zero || b || Mass remains the same.
M: A person in a freely falling lift experiences: || Double weight || Weightlessness || Normal weight || Increased mass || b || Apparent weight becomes zero.
M: Work done is zero when the force and displacement are: || In the same direction || Perpendicular || Opposite || Parallel || b || W = F·s·cos 90° = 0.
M: The pressure exerted by a liquid increases with: || Depth || Surface area || Volume of container || Shape of container || a || That is why dams are thicker at the base.
M: Ice floats on water because: || Ice is heavier || Ice is less dense than water || Water has surface tension || Ice is a solid || b || Density of ice ≈ 0.92 g/cm³.
M: Rise of oil in a lamp wick is due to: || Viscosity || Capillary action || Diffusion || Gravity || b || Surface tension effect.
M: The viscosity of liquids, with an increase in temperature: || Increases || Decreases || Remains the same || First increases then decreases || b || The viscosity of gases increases.
M: Woollen clothes keep us warm because: || Wool produces heat || Trapped air is a poor conductor || Wool absorbs sunlight || Wool is waterproof || b || Air is an insulator.
M: A thermos flask reduces heat loss by: || Conduction only || Convection only || Conduction, convection and radiation || Radiation only || c || Vacuum + silvered walls.
M: Echo is heard clearly when the reflecting surface is at least: || 5 m away || 17 m away || 50 m away || 100 m away || b || Persistence of hearing ≈ 0.1 s; speed of sound ≈ 340 m/s.
M: Speed of sound in air increases with: || Increase in pressure || Increase in temperature || Decrease in humidity || Decrease in temperature || b || Independent of pressure at constant temperature.
M: Infrasonic sounds are produced by: || Bats || Earthquakes and elephants || Dog whistles || Dolphins || b || Below 20 Hz.
M: The image formed by a plane mirror is: || Real and inverted || Virtual, erect, laterally inverted || Real and magnified || Virtual and diminished || b || Same size as the object.
M: The phenomenon responsible for a mirage is: || Diffraction || Total internal reflection (refraction in hot air) || Scattering || Dispersion || b || Hot air near the ground bends light.
M: The splitting of white light into seven colours is called: || Reflection || Refraction || Dispersion || Scattering || c || Newton's prism experiment; violet deviates most.
M: A rainbow is formed due to: || Dispersion, refraction and total internal reflection in raindrops || Scattering only || Diffraction || Polarisation || a || Seen opposite to the Sun.
M: Hypermetropia is corrected by a: || Concave lens || Convex lens || Cylindrical lens || Plane glass || b || The image forms behind the retina.
M: The least distance of distinct vision for a normal eye is: || 10 cm || 25 cm || 50 cm || 1 m || b || The far point is at infinity.
M: The resistance of a conductor increases with: || Increase in area || Increase in length || Decrease in temperature (metals) || Decrease in length || b || R = ρL/A.
M: The device that converts AC into DC is a: || Transformer || Rectifier || Inverter || Generator || b || An inverter converts DC to AC.
M: An ammeter is connected in a circuit in: || Parallel || Series || Either || Neither || b || A voltmeter is connected in parallel.
M: The magnetic effect of electric current was discovered by: || Faraday || Oersted || Ampere || Maxwell || b || 1820.
M: Electromagnetic induction was discovered by: || Oersted || Michael Faraday || Tesla || Edison || b || Basis of generators and transformers.
M: Which rays have the shortest wavelength? || X-rays || Gamma rays || Ultraviolet || Microwaves || b || Most energetic EM waves.
M: Microwave ovens heat food by agitating: || Fat molecules || Water molecules || Salt || Proteins only || b || Microwaves are absorbed by water.
M: LASER stands for Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of: || Rays || Radiation || Rotation || Resonance || b || Produces coherent, monochromatic light.
M: Carbon-14 dating is used to find the age of: || Rocks billions of years old || Fossils and archaeological remains || Stars || Meteorites || b || Half-life ≈ 5,730 years.
M: Control rods in a nuclear reactor are made of: || Graphite || Cadmium or boron || Uranium || Heavy water || b || They absorb neutrons; graphite and heavy water are moderators.

## 8.2 Chemistry
M: The fourth state of matter is: || Bose–Einstein condensate || Plasma || Colloid || Liquid crystal || b || Found in the Sun, lightning and neon signs.
M: Which of these shows the Tyndall effect? || Salt solution || Sugar solution || Milk || Pure water || c || Colloids scatter light.
M: Which method is used to separate the components of petroleum? || Crystallisation || Fractional distillation || Sublimation || Filtration || b || Different boiling points.
M: Chromatography is used to separate: || Cream from milk || Dyes and pigments || Salt from sea water || Iron from sand || b || Based on different adsorption.
M: Elements with the same mass number but different atomic numbers are called: || Isotopes || Isobars || Isotones || Allotropes || b || E.g., ⁴⁰Ar and ⁴⁰Ca.

M: Halogens belong to group: || 1 || 2 || 16 || 17 || d || Fluorine, chlorine, bromine, iodine, astatine.
M: Which gas is used in balloons and by deep-sea divers? || Hydrogen || Helium || Neon || Argon || b || Non-flammable; mixed with oxygen for divers.
M: Which gas is filled in electric bulbs? || Oxygen || Argon || Hydrogen || Carbon dioxide || b || Inert; prevents filament oxidation.
M: The reaction of an acid with a base is called: || Oxidation || Neutralisation || Hydrolysis || Saponification || b || Produces salt and water.
M: Saponification is the process of making: || Glass || Soap || Cement || Paper || b || Fat + alkali (NaOH) → soap + glycerol.
M: Which acid is known as the 'King of Chemicals'? || Hydrochloric acid || Sulphuric acid || Nitric acid || Acetic acid || b || Also called oil of vitriol.
M: Milk of magnesia is used as an: || Antiseptic || Antacid || Antibiotic || Analgesic || b || Magnesium hydroxide.
M: Rusting of iron requires: || Only oxygen || Only water || Oxygen and water || Carbon dioxide || c || Forms hydrated ferric oxide.
M: Which catalyst is used in the hydrogenation of vegetable oils? || Iron || Nickel || Platinum || Vanadium pentoxide || b || Produces vanaspati ghee.
M: The Haber process manufactures: || Sulphuric acid || Ammonia || Nitric acid || Urea || b || Iron catalyst.
M: Epsom salt is: || Sodium sulphate || Magnesium sulphate || Calcium sulphate || Zinc sulphate || b || Used as a laxative.
M: Gypsum is added to cement to: || Increase strength || Slow down setting || Change colour || Make it waterproof || b || Calcium sulphate dihydrate.
M: Which gas is responsible for acid rain? || Nitrogen || Sulphur dioxide || Oxygen || Helium || b || Along with nitrogen oxides; damages the Taj Mahal ('marble cancer').
M: Carbon monoxide is poisonous because it: || Destroys lungs || Combines with haemoglobin || Lowers blood sugar || Damages kidneys || b || Binds about 200 times more strongly than oxygen.
M: Itai-itai disease is caused by poisoning from: || Mercury || Cadmium || Lead || Arsenic || b || Minamata – mercury.
M: Which glass is used in laboratory apparatus because it resists heat? || Soda-lime glass || Pyrex (borosilicate) || Flint glass || Crown glass || b || Low thermal expansion.
M: Which oxide gives glass a blue colour? || Chromium oxide || Cobalt oxide || Manganese dioxide || Cuprous oxide || b || Chromium – green; manganese – violet; cuprous – red.

M: 'Rayon' is also called: || Artificial wool || Artificial silk || Synthetic rubber || Glass fibre || b || Made from cellulose.
M: Kevlar is used in: || Non-stick pans || Bulletproof vests || Water pipes || Electrical switches || b || Very strong aramid fibre.
