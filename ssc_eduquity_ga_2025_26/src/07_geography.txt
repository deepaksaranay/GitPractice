# Important MCQs – Geography

## 7.1 India
M: The southernmost point of India is: || Kanyakumari || Indira Point || Rameswaram || Dhanushkodi || b || On Great Nicobar. Kanyakumari is the southern tip of the mainland.
M: Kibithu, the easternmost village of India, is in: || Sikkim || Arunachal Pradesh || Nagaland || Manipur || b || Anjaw district.
M: Which line separates India and China in the eastern sector? || Radcliffe Line || McMahon Line || Durand Line || Line of Control || b || Drawn at the Simla Conference, 1914.
M: Which Indian state has the longest coastline? || Andhra Pradesh || Gujarat || Tamil Nadu || Maharashtra || b || About 1,600 km.
M: Which is the smallest Union Territory of India by area? || Chandigarh || Lakshadweep || Puducherry || Daman & Diu || b || Ladakh is the largest UT.
M: The capital of Andaman and Nicobar Islands is now called: || Port Blair || Sri Vijaya Puram || Car Nicobar || Havelock || b || Renamed in 2024.
M: Which hill range lies between the Narmada and Tapi rivers? || Vindhya || Satpura || Aravalli || Ajanta || b || Dhupgarh (Pachmarhi) is its highest peak.
M: The Palghat (Palakkad) Gap connects: || Kerala and Tamil Nadu || Karnataka and Goa || Maharashtra and Gujarat || Odisha and Andhra || a || A break in the Western Ghats.
M: The 'Nilgiri Hills' are the meeting point of: || Aravallis and Vindhyas || Western and Eastern Ghats || Satpura and Vindhya || Himalaya and Purvanchal || b || Doddabetta is the highest peak.
M: Which river is known as the 'Dakshin Ganga'? || Kaveri || Godavari || Krishna || Mahanadi || b || Kaveri is also sometimes called so in the south; Godavari is the standard SSC answer.
M: The Kaveri river originates at: || Mahabaleshwar || Talakaveri (Brahmagiri) || Amarkantak || Nasik || b || Karnataka; Shivasamudram falls on its course.
M: The Krishna river originates near: || Trimbakeshwar || Mahabaleshwar || Amarkantak || Kodagu || b || Tributaries Tungabhadra, Bhima, Koyna.
M: The Chambal river is known for its: || Delta || Badlands (ravines) || Waterfalls || Lakes || b || Also the National Chambal Gharial Sanctuary.
M: Which river flows into the Rann of Kutch? || Sabarmati || Luni || Mahi || Narmada || b || The main river of the Thar.
M: Which is the longest river of Kerala? || Periyar || Bharathapuzha || Pamba || Chaliyar || a || Idukki arch dam is on it.
M: Chitrakote Falls, called the 'Niagara of India', is on the: || Narmada || Indravati || Mahanadi || Godavari || b || Bastar, Chhattisgarh.
M: The Bhakra Nangal dam is built on the: || Beas || Sutlej || Ravi || Chenab || b || Reservoir: Gobind Sagar.
M: Mettur dam is built on the river: || Krishna || Kaveri || Godavari || Tungabhadra || b || Tamil Nadu.
M: The Nagarjuna Sagar dam is on the: || Godavari || Krishna || Kaveri || Mahanadi || b || Telangana–Andhra Pradesh.
M: Pong Dam is built on the: || Sutlej || Beas || Ravi || Yamuna || b || Himachal Pradesh; Maharana Pratap Sagar.
M: 'Loo' is a hot, dry wind blowing in: || Coastal Kerala || North Indian plains in summer || Assam in winter || Ladakh || b || May–June.
M: 'Mango showers' occur in: || Punjab || Kerala and Karnataka || Rajasthan || Bihar || b || Pre-monsoon showers that help mangoes ripen.
M: The western disturbances bring winter rain to: || South India || North-west India || North-east India || Coastal Odisha || b || Beneficial for rabi wheat.
M: Which soil covers the largest area in India? || Black || Alluvial || Red || Laterite || b || About 40–43% of the area.
M: 'Usar', 'Reh' and 'Kallar' are local names of: || Laterite soil || Saline and alkaline soil || Peaty soil || Desert soil || b || Treated with gypsum.
M: The 'sundari' tree is found in: || Thar desert || Sundarbans mangroves || Western Ghats || Himalayas || b || Gives the Sundarbans its name.
M: Which state has the largest forest cover by area? || Arunachal Pradesh || Madhya Pradesh || Chhattisgarh || Odisha || b || Mizoram has the highest percentage.
M: Eravikulam National Park is famous for the: || Lion-tailed macaque || Nilgiri tahr || Hangul || Red panda || b || Kerala; Neelakurinji blooms every 12 years.
M: Namdapha National Park is in: || Assam || Arunachal Pradesh || Sikkim || Meghalaya || b || Home to tiger, leopard, snow leopard and clouded leopard.
M: Gahirmatha is famous for: || Royal Bengal tigers || Olive Ridley turtles || Flamingos || Wild ass || b || Odisha coast.
M: The Indian wild ass is found in the: || Thar Desert NP || Little Rann of Kutch || Kanha || Hemis || b || Gujarat.
M: The largest tiger reserve in India is: || Jim Corbett || Nagarjunsagar–Srisailam || Sundarbans || Kanha || b || Andhra Pradesh–Telangana.
M: The first Ramsar sites of India (1981) were: || Wular and Loktak || Chilika and Keoladeo || Sambhar and Vembanad || Sundarbans and Pulicat || b || Tamil Nadu now has the most Ramsar sites.
M: Mumbai High is known for: || Coal || Offshore oil || Iron ore || Uranium || b || Discovered in 1974.
M: Neyveli in Tamil Nadu is known for: || Gold || Lignite || Copper || Mica || b || Neyveli Lignite Corporation.
M: Khetri in Rajasthan is famous for: || Zinc || Copper || Lead || Gold || b || Zawar – zinc and lead.
M: Panna in Madhya Pradesh is famous for: || Coal || Diamonds || Bauxite || Manganese || b || India's only active diamond mine (NMDC, Majhgawan).
M: Kudankulam nuclear power plant is built with the help of: || France || Russia || USA || Japan || b || Tamil Nadu; India's largest nuclear plant.
M: Bhadla Solar Park is in: || Gujarat || Rajasthan || Karnataka || Madhya Pradesh || b || Jodhpur district; one of the world's largest.
M: The first train of India ran on: || 16 April 1853 || 15 August 1854 || 1 January 1850 || 26 January 1853 || a || Bori Bunder (Bombay) to Thane, 34 km.
M: The headquarters of the Northeast Frontier Railway is at: || Guwahati (Maligaon) || Siliguri || Dibrugarh || Shillong || a || East Central Railway – Hajipur.
M: India's first metro rail began in: || Delhi || Kolkata || Mumbai || Chennai || b || 1984.
M: The Atal Tunnel is located at: || Banihal || Rohtang || Zojila || Nathu La || b || Himachal Pradesh, opened 2020.
M: Mormugao port is mainly used for exporting: || Coal || Iron ore || Tea || Cotton || b || Goa.

## 7.2 World and Physical Geography
M: The Sun's light reaches the Earth in about: || 8 seconds || 8 minutes 20 seconds || 1 hour || 1.3 seconds || b || Moonlight takes about 1.3 s.
M: Which planet rotates on its side? || Saturn || Uranus || Neptune || Venus || b || Axial tilt about 98°.
M: The asteroid belt lies between: || Earth and Mars || Mars and Jupiter || Jupiter and Saturn || Venus and Earth || b || Ceres is a dwarf planet in the belt.
M: A lunar eclipse occurs when: || Moon comes between Sun and Earth || Earth comes between Sun and Moon || Sun comes between Earth and Moon || Moon is at perigee only || b || Only on a full moon.
M: The Equator does NOT pass through: || Brazil || Kenya || Indonesia || India || d || It passes through 13 countries including Ecuador, Colombia, Kenya, Indonesia.
M: The International Date Line roughly follows: || 0° || 90°E || 180° || 23.5°N || c || Crossing it westward adds a day.
M: The SIAL layer of the Earth's crust is rich in: || Silica and magnesium || Silica and aluminium || Nickel and iron || Iron and magnesium || b || Continental crust. SIMA – oceanic; NIFE – core.
M: Marble is a metamorphic form of: || Sandstone || Limestone || Shale || Granite || b || Shale → slate; sandstone → quartzite.
M: Which seismic waves cannot pass through liquids? || P-waves || S-waves || L-waves || Surface waves || b || Evidence for a liquid outer core.
M: The 'Ring of Fire' surrounds the: || Atlantic Ocean || Pacific Ocean || Indian Ocean || Arctic Ocean || b || About 75% of active volcanoes.
M: Which layer of the atmosphere reflects radio waves? || Troposphere || Stratosphere || Ionosphere || Exosphere || c || Part of the thermosphere.
M: Nitrogen makes up about what percent of dry air? || 21% || 50% || 78% || 1% || c || Oxygen ≈ 21%, argon ≈ 0.93%.
M: 'Roaring Forties' are strong winds of the: || Northern Hemisphere || Southern Hemisphere || Equatorial belt || Polar regions only || b || Westerlies at 40°–50°S.
M: Harmattan, a dry dusty wind, blows in: || West Africa || Australia || Argentina || Italy || a || Called 'the doctor' for its cooling, dry air.
M: The Humboldt (Peru) current is a: || Warm current || Cold current || Tidal current || River current || b || Rich fishing grounds; weakened during El Niño.
M: The saltiest major water body among these is the: || Red Sea || Dead Sea || Baltic Sea || Arabian Sea || b || Dead Sea (≈ 340‰); also the lowest point on land.
M: Lake Victoria is the source of the: || Blue Nile || White Nile || Congo || Niger || b || Largest lake of Africa.
M: Angel Falls, the highest waterfall, is in: || Brazil || Venezuela || Peru || Canada || b || Over 900 m.
M: The Andes are the longest mountain range in: || Asia || North America || South America || Europe || c || Along the western coast.
M: The Strait of Malacca separates: || India and Sri Lanka || Malaysia (Malay Peninsula) and Sumatra || Spain and Morocco || Russia and USA || b || A key shipping route between the Indian and Pacific Oceans.
M: The Bering Strait separates: || Asia and North America || Europe and Africa || Asia and Europe || Australia and Asia || a || Russia and Alaska (USA).
M: The Suez Canal connects the Mediterranean Sea with the: || Black Sea || Red Sea || Arabian Sea || Caspian Sea || b || Opened in 1869.
M: The Durand Line is the boundary between: || India and Pakistan || Pakistan and Afghanistan || India and China || India and Nepal || b || Drawn in 1893.
M: The 'Steppes' are grasslands of: || North America || Eurasia || South Africa || Australia || b || Veld – South Africa; Downs – Australia.
M: The parliament of Norway is called the: || Riksdag || Storting || Folketing || Althing || b || Riksdag – Sweden; Folketing – Denmark; Althing – Iceland.
M: Naypyidaw is the capital of: || Thailand || Myanmar || Cambodia || Laos || b || Since 2005.
M: The currency of Bangladesh is the: || Rupee || Taka || Kyat || Rufiyaa || b || Kyat – Myanmar; Rufiyaa – Maldives.
