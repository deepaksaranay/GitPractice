# Solved Numericals – Set 1 (Statics to Pipe Flow)
SSC-style problems solved step by step (no options). g = 9.81 m/s², ρ_water = 1000 kg/m³ unless stated.

## 17.1 Properties
### Problem 1 – Viscosity conversion
An oil has dynamic viscosity 6 poise and specific gravity 0.9. Find μ in SI and ν in stokes.
F: μ = 6 × 0.1 = 0.6 Pa·s;  ρ = 900 kg/m³;  ν = 0.6/900 = 6.67 × 10⁻⁴ m²/s = 6.67 stokes
### Problem 2 – Shear between plates
Two plates 2 mm apart; upper plate moves at 1 m/s with oil (μ = 0.5 Pa·s) between them. Find the shear stress and force on a 0.5 m² plate.
F: τ = μV/h = 0.5 × 1/0.002 = 250 N/m²;  F = τA = 125 N
### Problem 3 – Journal bearing power loss
Shaft D = 100 mm, bearing length 200 mm, clearance 0.5 mm, N = 600 rpm, μ = 0.1 Pa·s. Find the power lost.
F: u = πDN/60 = π × 0.1 × 600/60 = 3.142 m/s;  τ = μu/t = 0.1 × 3.142/0.0005 = 628.3 N/m²
F: F = τ × πDL = 628.3 × π × 0.1 × 0.2 = 39.48 N;  T = F × D/2 = 1.974 N·m;  P = Tω = 1.974 × 62.83 = 124 W
### Problem 4 – Bulk modulus
Pressure on 1 m³ of water increases from 1 bar to 101 bar; its volume decreases by 0.5%. Find K.
F: K = Δp/(ΔV/V) = 100 × 10⁵ / 0.005 = 2 × 10⁹ Pa = 2 GPa
### Problem 5 – Soap bubble
Find the pressure inside a 40 mm soap bubble (σ = 0.04 N/m) above atmospheric.
F: Δp = 8σ/d = 8 × 0.04/0.04 = 8 N/m²
### Problem 6 – Capillary rise
Find the rise of water in a 2 mm glass tube (σ = 0.075 N/m, θ = 0).
F: h = 4σ/(ρgd) = 4 × 0.075/(1000 × 9.81 × 0.002) = 0.0153 m = 15.3 mm
### Problem 7 – Mercury depression
Find the depression of mercury in a 3 mm tube (σ = 0.52 N/m, θ = 130°, S = 13.6).
F: h = 4σ cosθ/(ρgd) = 4 × 0.52 × (−0.643)/(13600 × 9.81 × 0.003) = −0.0033 m → 3.3 mm depression

## 17.2 Statics and Hydrostatic Forces
### Problem 8 – Pressure at depth
Find the gauge pressure 5 m below the surface of oil (S = 0.8).
F: p = ρgh = 800 × 9.81 × 5 = 39.24 kPa;  head of water = 4 m
### Problem 9 – Hydraulic press
Plunger diameter 30 mm, ram diameter 300 mm, force on plunger 500 N. Find the load lifted.
F: W = F (D/d)² = 500 × 100 = 50 kN
### Problem 10 – U-tube manometer
A U-tube with mercury is connected to a water pipe. Pipe centre is 0.15 m above the mercury surface in the left limb; deflection 0.25 m. Find pipe pressure.
F: p + 1000 × 9.81 × 0.15 = 13600 × 9.81 × 0.25 → p = 33354 − 1471.5 = 31.88 kPa (gauge)
### Problem 11 – Differential manometer
A mercury differential manometer connected to two points of a horizontal water pipe reads 0.1 m. Find the pressure difference.
F: Δp = gx(ρm − ρ) = 9.81 × 0.1 × 12600 = 12.36 kPa;  head = 1.26 m of water
### Problem 12 – Absolute pressure
A vacuum gauge reads 30 kPa; atmospheric pressure is 101.3 kPa. Find absolute pressure.
F: p_abs = 101.3 − 30 = 71.3 kPa
### Problem 13 – Force on vertical gate
A rectangular gate 3 m wide holds water 4 m deep. Find the total force and its location.
F: F = ρgbH²/2 = 9810 × 3 × 16/2 = 235.4 kN;  acts at H/3 = 1.33 m above the base (2.67 m below surface)
### Problem 14 – Circular plate
A circular plate of 2 m diameter is immersed vertically with its top 1 m below the water surface. Find F and centre of pressure.
F: A = 3.142 m²;  h̄ = 2 m;  F = 9810 × 3.142 × 2 = 61.6 kN
F: I_G = πd⁴/64 = 0.785 m⁴;  h* = 2 + 0.785/(3.142 × 2) = 2.125 m
### Problem 15 – Inclined plate
A rectangular plate 1 m × 2 m (2 m along the slope) is inclined at 30° to the horizontal with its upper edge 1 m below the surface. Find F.
F: Depth of centroid h̄ = 1 + 1 × sin30° = 1.5 m;  F = 9810 × 2 × 1.5 = 29.4 kN
### Problem 16 – Curved surface
A quarter-circle gate of radius 2 m, 1 m wide, holds water up to its top. Find F_H, F_V and resultant.
F: F_H = ρg × (2 × 1) × 1 = 19.62 kN;  F_V = ρg × (π × 2²/4) × 1 = 30.82 kN
F: F = √(19.62² + 30.82²) = 36.5 kN at tan⁻¹(30.82/19.62) = 57.5° to horizontal
### Problem 17 – Rotating vessel
An open cylinder of 0.6 m diameter rotates at 120 rpm about its axis. Find the height of the paraboloid.
F: ω = 12.57 rad/s;  h = ω²R²/(2g) = 158 × 0.09/19.62 = 0.725 m;  rise at wall = fall at centre = 0.362 m
### Problem 18 – Accelerating tank
A tank accelerates horizontally at 2.45 m/s². Find the slope of the free surface.
F: tanθ = a/g = 0.25 → θ = 14.04°

## 17.3 Buoyancy and Stability
### Problem 19 – Fraction submerged
A wooden log (S = 0.7) floats in water. What fraction is above water?
F: Submerged fraction = 0.7 → 30% above water
### Problem 20 – Metacentric height of a block
A block 2 m wide, 4 m long, 1 m high (S = 0.6) floats with the 1 m side vertical. Find GM (rolling about the long axis).
F: Draft d = 0.6 m;  BM = b²/(12d) = 4/(7.2) = 0.556 m
F: OB = 0.3 m, OG = 0.5 m, BG = 0.2 m;  GM = 0.556 − 0.2 = 0.356 m (stable)
### Problem 21 – Experimental GM
A 50 kN weight moved 6 m across a ship of 20,000 kN displacement tilts it by 1.5°. Find GM.
F: GM = wx/(W tanθ) = 50 × 6/(20000 × 0.02619) = 0.573 m

## 17.4 Kinematics and Dynamics
### Problem 22 – Continuity check
Is u = 2x, v = −2y a possible incompressible flow? Is it irrotational?
F: ∂u/∂x + ∂v/∂y = 2 − 2 = 0 → possible;  ω_z = ½(∂v/∂x − ∂u/∂y) = 0 → irrotational
### Problem 23 – Acceleration
Velocity field u = 2x, v = −2y. Find acceleration at (1, 1).
F: a_x = u ∂u/∂x = 2x × 2 = 4 at x = 1;  a_y = v ∂v/∂y = −2y × (−2) = 4;  |a| = 5.66 m/s²
### Problem 24 – Stream function discharge
Two streamlines have ψ = 2 and ψ = 5 m²/s. Find discharge per metre width between them.
F: q = 5 − 2 = 3 m³/s per m
### Problem 25 – Bernoulli
Water flows through a pipe from 300 mm to 150 mm diameter at Q = 0.1 m³/s. Pressure at the larger section is 200 kPa. Find pressure at the smaller section (same level, no loss).
F: V₁ = 0.1/0.0707 = 1.415 m/s;  V₂ = 0.1/0.01767 = 5.66 m/s
F: p₂ = 200000 + 500(1.415² − 5.66²) = 200000 − 15016 = 185 kPa
### Problem 26 – Pitot tube
A pitot tube in water shows a stagnation head 0.5 m above the static head. Find velocity (C_v = 0.98).
F: V = 0.98 √(2 × 9.81 × 0.5) = 0.98 × 3.13 = 3.07 m/s
### Problem 27 – Force on a bend
A 90° horizontal bend carries 0.2 m³/s of water in a 300 mm pipe at 100 kPa (neglect loss). Find the resultant force.
F: A = 0.0707 m²;  V = 2.83 m/s;  ρQV = 566 N;  pA = 7070 N
F: F_x = pA + ρQV = 7636 N;  F_y = 7636 N;  F = 7636√2 = 10.8 kN at 45°

## 17.5 Flow Measurement
### Problem 28 – Venturimeter
A 300 × 150 mm venturimeter shows 0.2 m mercury deflection with water. C_d = 0.97. Find Q.
F: h = 0.2 × 12.6 = 2.52 m;  A₁ = 0.0707, A₂ = 0.01767;  √(A₁² − A₂²) = 0.06845
F: Q = 0.97 × 0.0707 × 0.01767 × √(2 × 9.81 × 2.52)/0.06845 = 0.97 × 0.01825 × 7.03 = 0.1245 m³/s
### Problem 29 – Orifice coefficients
A 50 mm orifice under 4 m head discharges 9 L/s; jet diameter at vena contracta 40 mm. Find C_c, C_d, C_v.
F: C_c = (40/50)² = 0.64;  Q_th = 0.001963 × √(78.48) = 0.01739 m³/s;  C_d = 0.009/0.01739 = 0.518;  C_v = 0.518/0.64 = 0.81
### Problem 30 – Tank emptying
A tank of area 2 m² with water 4 m deep empties through a 50 mm orifice (C_d = 0.6). Find time to empty.
F: a = 0.001963 m²;  T = 2A√H/(C_d a √(2g)) = 2 × 2 × 2/(0.6 × 0.001963 × 4.429) = 8/0.005217 = 1533 s ≈ 25.6 min
### Problem 31 – Rectangular notch
Find Q over a rectangular notch 1.5 m long with head 0.3 m (C_d = 0.62).
F: Q = (2/3) × 0.62 × 1.5 × 4.429 × 0.3^1.5 = 2.746 × 0.1643 = 0.451 m³/s
### Problem 32 – V-notch
Find Q over a 90° V-notch with head 0.2 m (C_d = 0.6).
F: Q = (8/15) × 0.6 × 4.429 × 1 × 0.2^2.5 = 1.417 × 0.01789 = 0.0254 m³/s = 25.4 L/s
### Problem 33 – Error in head
A 1% error in measuring head over a V-notch causes what error in discharge?
F: dQ/Q = 2.5 × dH/H = 2.5%

## 17.6 Laminar and Pipe Flow
### Problem 34 – Laminar check and loss
Oil (ν = 1 × 10⁻⁴ m²/s, S = 0.9) flows at 1.5 m/s in a 100 mm pipe, 200 m long. Find h_f.
F: Re = 1.5 × 0.1/10⁻⁴ = 1500 (laminar);  f = 64/1500 = 0.0427
F: h_f = fLV²/(2gD) = 0.0427 × 200 × 2.25/(1.962) = 9.78 m of oil
### Problem 35 – Maximum velocity in laminar flow
Average velocity in a pipe is 0.6 m/s (laminar). Find the maximum velocity and the radius where u = V.
F: u_max = 2V = 1.2 m/s;  r = R/√2 = 0.707R
### Problem 36 – Darcy loss
Water flows 0.05 m³/s through a 200 mm pipe, 1 km long, f = 0.02. Find h_f.
F: h_f = fLQ²/(12.1 D⁵) = 0.02 × 1000 × 0.0025/(12.1 × 0.00032) = 0.05/0.003872 = 12.9 m
### Problem 37 – Sudden enlargement
Pipe diameter changes suddenly from 150 mm to 300 mm; Q = 0.05 m³/s. Find the loss.
F: V₁ = 2.83 m/s;  V₂ = 0.707 m/s;  h = (2.12)²/19.62 = 0.229 m
### Problem 38 – Power transmission
Water at 500 m head is transmitted through a pipe; friction loss is 100 m. Find efficiency of transmission and check maximum-power condition.
F: η = (500 − 100)/500 = 80%;  max power would need h_f = 500/3 = 166.7 m (η = 66.7%)
### Problem 39 – Water hammer
Water flowing at 1.5 m/s in a rigid pipe is stopped suddenly (K = 2.1 GPa). Find the pressure rise.
F: c = √(2.1 × 10⁹/1000) = 1449 m/s;  Δp = ρVc = 1000 × 1.5 × 1449 = 2.17 MPa
### Problem 40 – Equivalent pipe
Pipes 600 m of 0.3 m and 400 m of 0.2 m are in series. Find the equivalent length of a 0.3 m pipe.
F: L_e = 600 + 400 × (0.3/0.2)⁵ = 600 + 400 × 7.594 = 3638 m
